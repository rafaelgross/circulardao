# Legacy

Early prototype governance contract (`DAO.sol`) and its own deploy/demo
scripts, superseded by `contracts/core/CircularDAO.sol` (OpenZeppelin
Governor-based). Never deployed to Sepolia — not in `deployments/sepolia.json`
under any address, and no address ever held a role on any of the live
contracts except the deployer (revoked, C1) and `CircularDAO`. Kept here for
history, out of the compiled/active contract set.
